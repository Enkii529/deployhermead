Next up, UK publishers can block Google AI search.
UK publishers have gained the right to opt out of Google's AI-powered search results, a move by the Competition and Markets Authority that strengthens their negotiating position for content licensing deals. This decision could reshape how AI systems consume news content and sets an important precedent for intellectual property rights in the AI era. Publishers now have more leverage to demand compensation for the use of their work. (BBC, June 3)

Next up, Trump orders early access to AI models.
President Trump signed an executive order requiring AI developers to provide the U.S. government with early access to advanced models to assess cybersecurity risks and protect critical infrastructure. The order represents a more proactive federal stance on AI oversight, though its voluntary nature leaves questions about enforcement. This move could influence how companies approach model releases and government relations. (CNN, June 2)

Next up, Mathematicians warn AI threatens field.
A group of leading mathematicians has published the Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics, warning that AI could threaten the foundations of their profession. The declaration, signed by over 130 experts, responds to incidents such as OpenAI's claim that its AI disproved an 80-year-old geometry conjecture. It raises fundamental questions about the role of AI in fundamental research and the preservation of academic rigor. (Ars Technica, June 2)

Next up, Microsoft partners with Mayo on health AI.
Microsoft has partnered with the Mayo Clinic to improve the accuracy of health information generated by AI chatbots. As tens of millions of people turn to AI for medical advice, this collaboration aims to integrate Mayo Clinic's clinical expertise to ensure responses are reliable and evidence-based. The partnership addresses a critical challenge in AI deployment: maintaining safety in high-stakes domains. (CNN, June 2)

Thrive Holdings announced a $1 billion investment in an AI-powered accounting roll-up, signaling strong confidence in AI's ability to transform traditional finance operations.
